Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Barber Booking Bot provides appointment scheduling functionality for a barber business through WhatsApp and Google Calendar.

Information We Process

The service may process information necessary to manage appointments, including customer contact information, customer names, requested services, appointment dates and times, and scheduling-related messages.

Google Calendar Data

Barber Booking Bot accesses Google Calendar only for appointment scheduling purposes.

Google Calendar access may be used to:

  • Check calendar availability.
  • Create barber appointments.
  • Update or reschedule appointments.
  • Reflect appointment cancellations.
  • Prevent scheduling conflicts and double bookings.

Google Calendar data is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and maintain the appointment scheduling functionality described above.

Data Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the scheduling system, such as WhatsApp messaging infrastructure, cloud hosting, database hosting, and Google Calendar.

Personal information is not sold to advertisers.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect scheduling information and credentials.

Data Retention

Appointment and scheduling information may be retained as reasonably necessary to operate the booking service, maintain scheduling history, prevent conflicts, and troubleshoot service issues.

Your Choices

Customers may contact the barber business to request information about their appointments or to request cancellation or correction of appointment-related information.
