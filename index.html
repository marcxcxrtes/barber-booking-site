Barber Booking Bot

Barber Booking Bot is an appointment scheduling service used to help customers book, reschedule, and manage barber appointments through WhatsApp.

The service uses Google Calendar to check availability and maintain appointment scheduling information.

Access to Google Calendar is used only for scheduling-related purposes such as checking availability, creating appointments, updating appointment times, and reflecting cancellations.
